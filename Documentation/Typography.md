# Typography

## Font Stack

| Use | Font | Weight | Style | Case |
|-----|------|--------|-------|------|
| **Buttons** | Optic Medium Expanded | 500 | — | All caps |
| **Labels** | Optic Bold Expanded | 700 | — | All caps |
| **Body/Descriptions** | Cabin Medium | 500 | — | Sentence |
| **Headline** | Optic Heavy Expanded | 800 | — | All caps |
| **Subheader** | Metallophile Medium | 500 | — | All caps |

## CSS Classes

- `.font-button` — Optic Medium Expanded, uppercase
- `.font-label` — Optic Bold Expanded, uppercase
- `.font-body` — Cabin Medium
- `.font-headline` — Optic Heavy Expanded, uppercase
- `.font-subheader` — Metallophile Medium, uppercase

## Tailwind Font Families

- `font-optic` — Optic
- `font-metallophile` — Metallophile Sp8
- `font-cabin` — Cabin
